About Me

關於我

我是孫筱媛 Joy，擁有八年以上影音企劃、腳本撰寫、影音製作與剪輯經驗，具備概念發想到實際完成作品之實務能力。熟悉品牌影像策略、商業拍攝現場管理及影像器材維護管理。

除影像製作外，也負責團隊案件分配與進度協調，依據專案需求分工，並負責跨部門溝通、需求整合及執行問題協調，確保各項專案順利推進。

曾製作30餘集與捷運周邊景點相關的系列影片，含括範圍美食、旅遊資訊、古蹟文物等等，因此，擅長因應複雜場域與多元拍攝需求，並從日常生活中汲取創作靈感。

並主導「台北市長逛赤峰街」節目企劃與拍攝。因需配合市府行程，拍攝過程遭遇臨時異動，當下就與團隊調整腳本與拍攝安排，在無法重拍的情況下靈活應變，順利完成當集拍攝與製作。

近期也持續嘗試運用生成式AI(ChatGPT、Gemini)結合影像，讓生成式AI創造可以符合企劃方向之影片。配樂方面，則運用 Suno AI 生成適合影片風格與節奏的背景音樂，再搭配畫面進行剪輯與整合，讓整體視聽效果更加完整。

English

My name is Joy Sun. I have over 8 years of experience in video planning, scriptwriting, filming, and video editing. I can handle the whole video production process, from the first idea to the final video. I also have experience in brand video planning, managing commercial shoots, and taking care of filming equipment.

Besides video production, I also help manage team projects. I assign tasks, track project progress, and communicate with different departments. I work with team members to understand project needs, solve problems, and make sure projects run smoothly.

I have produced more than 30 videos introducing places near Taipei Metro stations, including local food, travel spots, and historical sites. These experiences have helped me work in different filming environments and handle different production needs. I also enjoy finding creative ideas in everyday life.

I also led the planning and filming of a video featuring the former Taipei Mayor visiting Chifeng Street. During filming, the schedule suddenly changed because we had to follow the city government's arrangements. My team and I quickly adjusted the script and filming plan. Since we had no chance to film again, we had to respond quickly and finish the video successfully.

Recently, I have been learning to use generative AI tools such as ChatGPT and Gemini to create video content that matches my project ideas. I also use Suno AI to create background music that fits the style and rhythm of each video. By combining AI-generated content, music, and video editing, I hope to make my videos more creative, realistic, and engaging.
